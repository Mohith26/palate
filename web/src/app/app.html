Rank every restaurant you've tried in about three taps.

Palate turns "which did you like more?" into a ranked list, a 0 to 10 score, and picks from friends who share your taste. Binary insertion inside a sentiment band, Kendall tau taste matching, and an mSPRT + CUPED experiment analyzer. Django REST + PostgreSQL backend, Angular frontend.
